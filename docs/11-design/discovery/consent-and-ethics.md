# Consent and Research Ethics

## Consent script (read aloud, adapt to the language)

"I am helping redesign a quiz app. I would like to ask about how you use apps with money. Your answers are used only to improve the app. You can skip any question and stop at any time without any consequence. I will write notes without your name. I would like to record audio so I do not miss anything; this is optional and the recording is deleted after notes are written. Are you 18 or older? Do you agree to continue?"

## Rules

- Participants are 18 or older and agree verbally; record the agreement in the notes.
- No names or phone numbers in notes; use P1 to P5.
- Notes are stored in the project's private drive, not in this repository. The synthesis (without personal details) goes in `synthesis.md`.
- Delete audio within 7 days.
- If a participant shows signs of harmful gambling, pause the session, thank them, and share a support contact appropriate for their country.
- Pay or thank participants regardless of how much they complete.
