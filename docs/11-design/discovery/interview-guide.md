# Player Interview Guide (story G-007)

Goal: understand how people use quiz-for-money apps today, what makes them trust or distrust them, and where the current IHUGURE flow confuses them. Five interviews of 30 minutes, in Kinyarwanda unless the participant prefers another language.

## Who to recruit (5 people)

| Mix | Why |
|---|---|
| 2 who already played IHUGURE | Real pain points of the current flow |
| 2 who play other mobile-money games or betting apps | Expectations and comparison |
| 1 who has never used one | First-time comprehension |

Include different phones (low-end Android at least once), different data situations, and at least one woman and one person outside Kigali. Participants must be 18 or older. Do not recruit people who describe harmful gambling habits for this study; if one appears, stop and give them support information.

## Before you start

- Read the consent script (consent-and-ethics.md) aloud and get a yes.
- Offer a small thank-you in airtime. No stake money is used in the sessions.
- Take notes only; record audio only with explicit consent.

## Questions

1. Warm-up: What do you do on your phone when you have 10 minutes? Which apps with money do you use?
2. Mobile money: Tell me about the last time you sent or received money. What worried you?
3. Games and quizzes: Have you played a quiz for money? Walk me through the last time.
4. Trust: What makes you trust or not trust an app that holds your money? What would make you leave?
5. Rules: How do you usually find out what you can win? What do you do when the rules are long?
6. Time pressure: How do you feel about 12 seconds per question? What would make it fairer?
7. Problems: Tell me about a time money did not arrive or a result felt wrong. What did you do?
8. Support: If something went wrong, who would you contact? How fast do you expect an answer?
9. Language: Which language do you want for rules and money screens?
10. Limits: How would you feel about setting a daily spending limit? When would that be helpful?

## Task tests (show the flow sketches, one at a time)

- Play: "Show me how you would start a game with 500."
- Rules: "What do you expect to win if you answer all three correctly? What if you get two?"
- Deposit: "How would you add money? What would you check before you confirm?"
- Withdraw: "How would you take your money out? What do you expect to see afterwards?"

Note where they hesitate, misread or ask a question. Do not help unless they are stuck for more than 30 seconds.

## What to capture per participant

Language, phone type, data situation, quotes (short, anonymised), the three biggest friction points, trust signals they named, and the screen elements they ignored.
