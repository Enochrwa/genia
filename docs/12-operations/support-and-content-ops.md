# Support and Content Operations

## Support

Channels: in-app tickets, WhatsApp/phone line, email. Targets: first response < 1 h in business hours, resolution < 24 h; payment issues prioritised.
Playbook for disputes: gather game ID and ledger entries, compare server records, decide, document, notify.
Macros per language.

## Content operations

Roles: writers, fact-checkers, translators, approvers. Weekly pipeline, monthly refresh, quality dashboard (correct rate, reports, response time), retire/flag rules.
