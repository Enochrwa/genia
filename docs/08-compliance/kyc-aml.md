# KYC and AML

## Tiers
| Tier | Requirement | Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Phone verified | Free play |
| 1 | Age declaration + verified phone | Small deposits and play within low limits |
| 2 | ID document + liveness (Smile Identity / Sumsub) | Withdrawals, higher limits |
| 3 | Enhanced due diligence | Large volumes |

## AML controls
Transaction monitoring, structuring detection, sanctions/PEP screening where required, suspicious-activity escalation to the compliance officer, record retention, staff training.

## Age
Hard 18+ enforcement through verification; block under-age accounts and report per local rules.
