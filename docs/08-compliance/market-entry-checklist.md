# Market Entry Checklist

- [ ] Written legal opinion on classification and licensing
- [ ] Licence/registration obtained or exemption confirmed
- [ ] Tax treatment (prizes, turnover, withholding) confirmed
- [ ] Payment methods and processors approved for this product
- [ ] KYC/AML requirements implemented
- [ ] Minimum age confirmed and enforced
- [ ] Responsible-gaming requirements implemented
- [ ] Terms, privacy, refund policies localised and reviewed
- [ ] Languages and currency configured
- [ ] Data protection obligations met
- [ ] Support channels and hours defined
- [ ] Country feature flag set to enabled (final step)
