# Privacy and Data Protection

- Data minimisation; purpose limitation; documented lawful basis.
- Privacy policy and consent screens localised.
- User rights: access, export, correction, deletion (subject to legal retention of financial records).
- Processor agreements with SMS, KYC, hosting, analytics vendors.
- Data residency decisions per market; breach notification procedure and timelines per law.
- Rwanda data protection law applies from day one; GDPR-style duties when serving EU/UK users.
- DPIA for KYC and fraud profiling.
