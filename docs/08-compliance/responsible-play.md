# Responsible Play

- Deposit, loss and time limits (user-set; decreases immediate, increases after cooling period).
- Cool-off and self-exclusion (24 h, 7 d, 30 d, permanent), enforced across accounts via phone/ID.
- Reality checks (session time and net result reminders).
- Clear risk statement and links to support organisations per country.
- Staff tools to restrict users showing harm indicators.
- No marketing to excluded users; no dark patterns around deposits.
