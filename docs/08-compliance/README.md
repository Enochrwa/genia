# Compliance Overview

> Not legal advice. Obtain written advice from qualified counsel in every target country before enabling real-money play.

## Gate
Real-money play is disabled until, per country: legal opinion obtained, licence (if required) held, payment provider approval, age/KYC flow active, responsible-play tools active, terms localised.

## Classification risk
Paid-entry games with cash prizes can be treated as gambling, lottery or regulated contests even if skill-based. Treatment varies by country and region. Where not permitted, offer free-to-play or non-cash modes, or do not launch.
