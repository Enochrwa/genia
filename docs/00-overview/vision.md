# Vision

**Genia** is a transparent, mobile-first, skill-based quiz platform. Players choose an entry level, answer timed questions, and see exactly what they can win before they pay. Results and payouts are verifiable and fast.

## Principles

1. **Transparency:** rules, fees and prizes shown before confirmation.
2. **Fairness:** server-authoritative games; difficulty fixed and disclosed before play.
3. **Trust:** receipts, history, reachable support.
4. **Safety:** responsible-play tools, age checks, fraud controls.
5. **Local first, global ready:** Rwanda first; every market added through legal gating, local payments and languages.
6. **Mobile first:** low-data, fast, installable.

## Success measures (first 12 months)

- Payout success rate above 99% and median withdrawal time under 5 minutes (mobile money).
- Zero ledger mismatches in daily reconciliation.
- Support first response under 1 hour in business hours.
- Day-30 retention above 25%.
